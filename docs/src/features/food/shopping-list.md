# Shopping List

The shopping list sits at the bottom of the **Foods** page, under your meal plans.

## Adding items

- **By hand:** type an item, optionally with a quantity and unit (for example `500` `g` `Beef mince`), and choose **Add**.
- **From a meal plan:** in **Meal Plans**, open a plan's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to shopping list**. One week of the plan is added: every food in it, with saved meals expanded into their ingredients and scaled to the portion planned, totalled per food and unit. If an unticked item for the same food and unit is already on the list, its quantity goes up instead of the item appearing twice.

## Using the list

- Tick an item off as you shop. Ticked items move to the bottom.
- **Copy list** copies the unticked items as a plain text list, ready to paste into a message or notes app.
- **Clear ticked** removes everything you have ticked off.

## Sharing

The shopping list is part of your diary data. A family member with **Manage Diary** permission can view, add and tick off items for you; one with **View Reports** can view it. See [Family & Friends Sharing](/features/family-friends-sharing).
