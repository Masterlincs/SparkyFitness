# Log by Voice or Text

Log a whole meal from one sentence. In the **Diary**, choose the microphone button next to **Add Food** on a meal.

1. Say or type what you ate, for example *two eggs, a slice of toast and a cup of tea*. The microphone uses the same voice settings as Ask Sparky: your browser's own speech recognition by default, or an AI transcription model if you chose one.
2. Choose **Find foods**. Each item is matched to a food and a portion:
   - your own saved foods first, then the [Intake24](/features/settings/intake24) and [CoFID](/features/settings/cofid) UK databases;
   - weights and volumes convert (*250 g beef mince*, *a pint of milk*);
   - household units and sizes pick the matching portion (*a slice of toast*, *a large banana*);
   - a plain count uses a medium portion (*two eggs*: 2 × medium egg, 50 g).
3. Check each row. Change the food, portion or amount, or untick anything you don't want.
4. Choose **Log**. Everything is added to that meal on the day you are viewing.

This needs no AI provider. Matching happens on your server with the built-in UK data.

## When something isn't recognised

Items with no match are left unticked. **Ask Sparky instead** opens the assistant with those items already typed in, so an AI provider can work them out. That step does need an AI service configured in Settings.

Foods from Intake24 and CoFID are logged as diary entries without being added to your food library. Foods you have saved are logged against your saved portion.
